Brand Equity

Session 4 · what makes a brand, how it guides customer choice, and when brand activism builds trust rather than backlash.

Idea Diagram Frameworks Apply

The core idea

A brand is a promise the market has learned to believe. It differentiates lookalike products, guides choice under time pressure, and licenses a price premium — that stored trust is brand equity, an asset built by consistency and spent by every broken promise. Activism is the highest-stakes deposit: taking a stand builds trust when it matches the brand’s actions, and burns it when it reads as costume. — after the CD57 Module 5 material

Put simply · Why you grab the same coffee cup without thinking.

In a supermarket aisle with forty coffees, you grab the one you always grab. No comparison, no reading labels. That shortcut — trust stored in a name — is the brand doing its job, and it is why the branded jar charges more than the identical one beside it.

That stored trust is brand equity: built slowly by kept promises, spent instantly by broken ones. And when a brand takes a public stand, customers check one thing: does the company actually live like that? If yes, trust compounds. If no, the internet does the auditing for free.

Named ideas to remember.

Functions of a Brand · CD57 Module 5
Differentiation · Choice heuristic · Risk reduction for the buyer
Customers use brands to decide faster and safer — the brand earns exactly as much as the deciding it saves.
Brand Equity · after Aaker & Keller
Awareness · Associations · Perceived quality · Loyalty
Equity is the gap between what your product commands and what an identical unbranded one would — an asset, so manage it like one.
Brand Activism · CD57 Module 5
Stand aligned with brand actions · Authenticity test · Trust dividend or backlash
A stand only builds trust when the company’s own behaviour already backs it — activism is proof, not positioning.

How to apply

Managing the brand as an asset.

  1. Write the promise in one line. What does the customer believe they get, every time? If teams answer differently, there is no brand yet.
  2. Audit the promise against experience. Every broken delivery, rude call or ugly renewal is a withdrawal from equity you paid media money to build.
  3. Measure the premium. Price your product against a private-label twin. The gap is your equity, in currency.
  4. Test any stand against your record. Before the campaign: would our own conduct survive the scrutiny the stand invites? If not, fix conduct first.
