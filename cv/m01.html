Integrated Marketing Communications

The self-paced opener · traditional vs digital media, storytelling and consumer psychology, and the KPIs that say whether any of it worked.

Idea Diagram Frameworks Apply

The core idea

Promotion walks a customer down a staircase: notice the product, want it, act. Traditional media buys reach; digital buys targeting and a measurable trail. The craft is consistency — branding, storytelling and psychology aligned so every touchpoint tells one story. The discipline is measurement: reach, engagement, conversion, return on ad spend. An ad that cannot report its KPI is a cost, not a strategy. — after the CD57 Module 1 material

Put simply · A market stall with a megaphone and a clipboard.

Promotion is the megaphone: first make people look, then make them care, then make them want, then make it easy to buy. Four different jobs — most bad ads try to do all four in one breath.

Digital added the clipboard: now you can count who looked, who clicked, who bought. If you are not counting, you are not marketing — you are decorating.

Frameworks in this module

Named ideas to remember.

AIDA Funnel · E. St. Elmo Lewis, 1898
Attention · Interest · Desire · Action
Each stage loses people — design a distinct message for the stage, not one ad that tries to do all four.
Integrated Marketing Communications · after Schultz
One positioning · Consistent story across channels · Coordinated timing
Customers meet the brand across many touchpoints but form one impression — inconsistency is self-inflicted noise.
Traditional vs Digital Media Economics · CD57 Module 1
TV: reach + emotion · Social: targeting + feedback loop · KPIs: reach, CTR, conversion, ROAS
Traditional buys awareness you cannot track; digital buys precision you can — the mix depends on which failure you can afford.

How to apply

Auditing a promotion plan.

  1. Name the funnel stage each asset targets. An awareness TV spot and a retargeting ad have different jobs. Grade each against its own job.
  2. Read three touchpoints side by side. Website, social ad, packaging. If a stranger cannot tell they are one brand, integration has failed.
  3. Demand a KPI per objective. Awareness → reach; consideration → engagement; sales → conversion and ROAS. Refuse vanity metrics.
  4. Check the psychology is earned. Storytelling works when the story is about the customer’s problem — not the founder’s journey.
