The course arc

Two blocks. Ten modules.

Value the project with NPV, price the risk with CAPM and WACC, then choose how to finance it. — the course, told in one line

Block I · Investment decisions

  • m.01
    The NPV Rule
    Discount every cash flow at the return investors could earn elsewhere at the same risk. Invest if the sum beats the outlay.
    open →
  • m.02
    Free Cash Flow
    NPV needs cash, not profit. Build the number from EBIT, strip the accounting, keep only what the project changes.
    open →
  • m.03
    IRR and Its Traps
    IRR agrees with NPV just often enough to be dangerous. Know the two conditions under which it lies.
    open →
  • m.04
    Bond Pricing & the Yield Curve
    A bond is a bundle of promised cash flows. Price each at its own spot rate; the YTM is just the blended average.
    open →

Block II · Risk, valuation & financing

  • m.05
    Beta and the CAPM
    Diversification kills firm-specific risk for free — so the market only pays you for the risk that survives.
    open →
  • m.06
    WACC
    One number blends what all your investors require — and it is the right discount rate only for projects that look like the firm.
    open →
  • m.07
    Enterprise DCF
    Value the operations first, then split the pie: enterprise value minus net debt is what the equity is worth.
    open →
  • m.08
    One WACC Is Never Enough
    Three cases, one lesson: the cost of capital is a construction, and every construction choice moves the answer.
    open →
  • m.09
    Capital Structure: MM to Trade-off
    Start where financing is irrelevant, then add taxes and distress back one at a time — the optimum lives between them.
    open →
  • m.10
    The Pecking Order
    Watch what firms do, not what the trade-off says: internal cash first, then debt, equity only as a last resort.
    open →
