The core idea

Heritage is the strategy asset most firms treat as decoration: the accumulated trust, stories and capabilities that competitors cannot buy. The same logic applies to careers — your formative background is a differentiator, not a bias to hide. The course closes by joining the two: purpose-driven leadership is heritage pointed at the future, for the firm and for you. — after the CD61 closing session

How to apply

Closing the course on yourself.

  1. Write the firm's inheritance list. What do you own because of your history that a well-funded entrant could not buy? That is strategy raw material.
  2. Separate engine from anchor. For each inherited practice: does it still create value, or only comfort? Retire the anchors deliberately.
  3. Draft your own origin story. Three formative experiences, the capabilities they built, and the purpose they point to. This is the personal brand, honestly sourced.
  4. Point it at the course project. The systemic challenge you chose in module 1 should be one your heritage — firm's or yours — equips you to lead.
