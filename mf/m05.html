The core idea

Bring your firm's vision, purpose, values, a front-line job description and your brand promise — then compare them with your closest competitor's. The exercise bites because the documents rarely agree: the brand promises warmth while the job ad rewards throughput. Culture is what behaviour repeats, and behaviour follows what the artefacts actually incentivise, not what the poster says. — after the CD61 session 3 brief

How to apply

Running the artefact audit on your own firm.

  1. Collect the five artefacts. Vision, purpose, values, one front-line job ad, the brand promise. Verbatim, not paraphrased.
  2. Do the same for your rival. The comparison exposes which of your words are category wallpaper and which are actually yours.
  3. Walk the chain for breaks. Does the job ad hire for the values? Could the person in that ad keep the promise? Mark every seam that leaks.
  4. Fix one artefact, not the poster. Change the job description or the incentive, then let repeated behaviour move the culture.
