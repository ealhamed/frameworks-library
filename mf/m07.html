Money as a System

live session 5 · Background Note: The Value of Money · cowrie to crypto · Rai stones exercise.

Idea Diagram Frameworks Apply

The core idea

Money is a system, not an object: a ledger of who owes what, made portable. It began as literal ledgers in Mesopotamia, hardened into coin, dissolved into bank entries, and is now re-becoming a ledger in digital form. Each step improved coordination and created a new failure mode — debasement, bank runs, cyber risk — because money's value rests on institutions and confidence, not on the token. — after Tavassoli, Background Note: The Value of Money

The hero diagram

Five thousand years, one loop.

The arc runs ledger to ledger: clay records of barley debts, stamped coins, paper claims on banks, and digital entries again. The technology changes; the trust problem is permanent.

Frameworks in this module

Named ideas to remember.

Four Functions of Money · monetary canon, via the Background Note
Medium of exchange · Unit of account · Store of value · Standard of deferred payment
Judge any monetary innovation — CBDC, stablecoin, loyalty point — against all four functions; most fail at least one quietly.
Money as Social Ledger · Rai stones · the group exercise
Value = community consensus on who owns what · The token can be immovable; the ledger moves · Trust and verification are the real infrastructure
Yap islanders traded ownership of stones too heavy to move — proof that money was always the ledger, never the object. Crypto rediscovered this, with electricity.
Innovation-Risk Exchange · Tavassoli, Background Note
Coinage: uniformity, then debasement · Bank money: elasticity, then runs · Digital money: efficiency, then systemic cyber risk
Every monetary upgrade solves a coordination constraint by creating a new fragility — ask what the new one is before adopting the upgrade.

How to apply

Thinking about money flows like a systems manager.

  1. Separate flow from stock. Income is a flow; wealth is a stock. Firms and households confuse them whenever a good year is spent as if permanent.
  2. Apply the four functions. For any new payment or store-of-value pitch, score it function by function against the boring bank account.
  3. Locate the ledger. Whoever maintains the ledger holds the power — banks, card networks, or a protocol. Know whose ledger your business sits on.
  4. Name the new fragility. Faster settlement, programmable payments, tokenised assets: each removes a friction that was also a shock absorber.
